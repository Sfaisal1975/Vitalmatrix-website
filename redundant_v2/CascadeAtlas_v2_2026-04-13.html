WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT

The Terrain Is Not Random. It Has a Map.

The CascadeAtlas™ shows five terrain convergence zones and the six directional cascade pathways between them. Each pathway has a confirmed direction, a speed classification, and an evidence tier. This is not a diagram of what can happen. It is a map of what does happen: and the intelligence VitalMatrix™ uses to tell you which direction the dysfunction is moving.

THE FIVE ZONES

Each Zone Is a Pattern, Not a Label

Zones cluster nodes by clinical terrain logic. A zone activation means the nodes within it are showing coordinated burden: not isolated symptoms.

Z1 · GOLD

Metabolic Energy Axis

N3 Energy · N6 (endocrine signalling)

The core metabolic zone. Two nodes. The simplest entry point into the terrain. Z1 burden often drives cascades to Z3 and Z5.

Z2 · TEAL

Resilience Network

N1 Assimilation · N2 Defence and Repair · N6 (endocrine signalling)

Three nodes. The gut-immune-communication interface. Z2 is the default TerrainLock™ entry point and the most common tiebreaker zone in terrain cascade analysis.

Z3 · PURPLE

Cardiovascular-Neural Axis

N5 Transport · N6 (endocrine signalling)

The cardiovascular-neural convergence. N5 Transport and N6 (endocrine signalling) converge. Z3 frequently receives cascade input from Z1 via S6 (UNIDIRECTIONAL) and from Z2 via S5.

Z4 · SAGE

Detoxification Trident

N2 Defence and Repair · N4 Biotransformation and Elimination

The only zone with no N6 (endocrine signalling). Two nodes. Pure detoxification pairing. The exception zone: Z4 breaks the endocrine signalling node pattern that defines all other zones.

Z5 · WARM GOLD

Hormonal Terrain Axis

N3 Energy · N4 Biotransformation and Elimination · N6 (endocrine signalling)

Three nodes. The most complex zone. Energy, biotransformation, and endocrine signalling converge. Activation threshold is lower than all other zones (32 internal vs 40): reflecting hormonal terrain sensitivity.

THE SIX STACKS

Direction. Speed. Evidence. Three Facts per Cascade.

Each stack has a confirmed direction, a KINETICS™ velocity classification, and an evidence tier. CascadeIQ™ identifies which stacks are active for each patient.

S1 Z2 → Z1 Resilience to Metabolic. The immune-gut cascade driving metabolic dysfunction. The most common stack in complex chronic presentations. Medium Mechanism Established, Cascade Emerging
S2 Z1 → Z5 Metabolic to Hormonal. Metabolic burden drives hormonal terrain disruption at speed. Weeks-to-months timeline. Fast Established
S3 Z4 → Z1 Detox to Metabolic. Biotransformation burden impairs metabolic energy over a long timeline. Months to years. Slow Emerging
S4 Theoretical Z5 → Z2 Hormonal to Resilience. S4 is classified Theoretical: it displays for completeness but is excluded from DRD™ cascade trace per clinical governance rules. Medium Theoretical
S5 Z2 → Z3 Resilience to Cardiovascular-Neural. The gut-cardiovascular cascade. TMAO data under ongoing review. Fast Emerging
S6 Unidirectional Z1 → Z3 ONLY Metabolic to Cardiovascular-Neural. S6 is strictly unidirectional: Z1 to Z3 only. Reverse direction is not clinically supported. Medium Established
TERRAINLOCK™

The Self-Perpetuating Loop

TerrainLock™ describes a specific cascade configuration where three active stacks create a self-perpetuating terrain dysfunction cycle. Z2 activates Z1 via S1. Z1 activates Z5 via S2. Z5 engages Z2 via S4 (Theoretical: excluded from DRD™ cascade trace). When conditions persist, the cascade pattern becomes self-reinforcing.

TerrainLock™ activation requires S1 Active + S2 Active + (S4 Active OR both Z5 and Z2 internal scores above 60). When active, standard single-zone intervention is insufficient. The loop must be addressed simultaneously at the entry point (Z2 by default) and the fastest stack.
