The Seven Nodes

Seven biological systems. The foundation of terrain intelligence.

N1

Assimilation

Z2

Nutrient absorption, gut barrier integrity, and the processing of dietary inputs. N1 dysfunction manifests as malabsorption, intestinal permeability, and dysbiotic terrain.

N2

Defence and Repair

Z2Z4

Immune regulation, inflammatory response, and tissue repair capacity. N2 appears in both Z2 and Z4, making it a shared node across the resilience and detoxification zones.

N3

Energy

Z1Z5

Mitochondrial function, ATP production, and metabolic output. N3 anchors both Z1 and Z5, making it the shared metabolic driver across the two most clinically significant zones.

N4

Biotransformation and Elimination

Z4Z5

Phase I and Phase II detoxification, conjugation pathways, and elimination. N4 bridges detoxification and hormonal terrain, reflecting the clinical link between toxic burden and hormonal disruption.

N5

Transport

Z3

Cardiovascular function, lymphatic flow, and the movement of nutrients, hormones, and waste products. N5 is the sole node in Z3, making the cardiovascular-neural zone entirely dependent on transport integrity.

N6

N6 (endocrine signalling)

Z1 Z2 Z3 Z5 ★ 4 Zones

The dominant anchor node. N6 (endocrine signalling) appears in four of five zones: Z1, Z2, Z3, and Z5. HPA axis, HPT axis, neurotransmitter regulation, insulin signalling, and vagal afferents all fall within N6 terrain. Z4 is the only zone with no N6 component.

N7

Structural Integrity

Phase 2

N7 Structural Integrity is deferred to Phase 2. It is not scored in Phase 1. Do not reference N7 as active or available.

MES — Metabolic Environment and Substrate: foundational context for all seven nodes. Not a node. Not scored. Not displayed in zone calculations.

The Five Zones

Five terrain convergence zones. Activation thresholds. Compositions.

VitalMatrix™ generates working documents for practitioner review. All clinical decisions remain with the registered practitioner.

Z1 — Metabolic Energy Axis

N3 Energy + N6 (endocrine signalling)

Activation threshold: 40

Z1 is the metabolic anchor of the NCZ™ architecture. When both N3 and N6 are burdened simultaneously, the zone crosses its threshold and begins driving downstream cascade activity. Z1 is the most frequent Driver zone in clinical practice.

Z2 — Resilience Network

N1 Assimilation + N2 Defence and Repair + N6 (endocrine signalling)

Activation threshold: 40

Z2 integrates the gut, immune, and communication nodes. Z2 burden is a common upstream driver of Z1 suppression. The three-node composition makes Z2 the most complex zone to activate but also the most clinically informative when active.

Z3 — Cardiovascular-Neural Axis

N5 Transport + N6 (endocrine signalling)

Activation threshold: 40

Z3 composite scoring is not active in Phase 1. Z3 appears in the cascade architecture as a receiver zone: S5 (FAST, Emerging) from Z2, and S6 UNIDIRECTIONAL (MEDIUM, Established) from Z1.

Z3 is primarily a receiver zone in Phase 1. From Z1, N3 leaves. N5 (Transport) enters. Z3 receives cascade input from Z1 via S6 (UNIDIRECTIONAL) and from Z2 via S5.

Z4 — Detoxification Trident

N2 Defence and Repair + N4 Biotransformation and Elimination

Activation threshold: 40

Z4 composite scoring is not active in Phase 1. Z4 appears as the origin zone for S3: Detoxification drives Metabolic Energy (SLOW, Emerging).

Z4 is the only zone with no N6 (endocrine signalling) node. This is the memory anchor: Z4 breaks the pattern. Two-Four Break. No N6.

Z5 — Hormonal Terrain Axis

N6 (endocrine signalling) + N4 Biotransformation and Elimination + N3 Energy

Activation threshold: 32 — lower threshold: hormonal sensitivity

Z5 carries the lowest activation threshold in the NCZ™ framework: 32 versus 40 for Z1 through Z4. Z5 is almost always a Reactor zone. Z5 engages Z2 via S4 (Theoretical — excluded from DRD™ cascade trace). Three nodes: the most complex zone composition.

Memory Method

Five zones. Five stories. One architecture.

Z1 — Metabolic Energy Axis

Three-Six Core. Energy and Communication. The simplest zone. Two nodes. The metabolic core.

Z2 — Resilience Network

One-Two-Six Expansion. Z1 plus Assimilation and Defence. One joins the core pair. The Resilience Network builds on Z1.

Z3 — Cardiovascular-Neural Axis

Five-Six Shift. From Z1, N3 leaves. N5 (Transport) enters. The zone shifts from metabolic to vascular-neural.

Z4 — Detoxification Trident

Two-Four Break. No N6. The only zone with no Communication node. This is how you remember it: Z4 breaks the pattern.

Z5 — Hormonal Terrain Axis

Three-Four-Six Build. Energy returns, Biotransformation joins, Communication completes. The most complex zone. Three nodes. Hormonal terrain requires all three pillars.
