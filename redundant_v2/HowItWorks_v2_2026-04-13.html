The FLINT™ Framework

Five Layers of Terrain Intelligence

Each consultation flows through five sequential layers of analysis. Every layer asks a different clinical question and builds on the one before it.

I

WHERE?

Node Intelligence

Identifies which of the seven functional nodes carry the highest burden. Symptoms, medications, and history are mapped to their mechanistic terrain origins.

II

WHICH?

Zone Intelligence

Node scores composite into five clinical zones, revealing which terrain systems are most compromised and where clinical attention should focus.

III

HOW?

Cascade Intelligence

Detects cross-zone cascade stacks: the pathways through which burden in one zone propagates to another, amplifying clinical complexity.

IV

WHO?

Causality Intelligence

Determines which zone carries the highest burden of downstream dysfunction and which zones are reacting, guiding the practitioner to the root of the terrain disturbance.

V

WHAT / WHEN?

Examination Intelligence

Recommends targeted investigations and intervention sequencing based on cascade priority, enabling evidence-informed clinical planning.

Methodology

How the Terrain Intelligence Works

Patient intake data flows through the FLINT™ five-layer pipeline. Layer 1 scores seven biological nodes. Layer 2 composites those into five NCZ™ zones. Layer 3 detects cascade stacks between active zones. Layer 4 designates the Driver. Layer 5 generates budget-tiered investigation recommendations via APEX™. The entire pipeline executes in sequence, each layer building on the output of the one before it.
DeltaScan™ compares terrain assessments across time. It tracks zone score movement, cascade activation changes, and Driver designation shifts between consultations. This gives the practitioner a longitudinal view of how the terrain is responding to intervention.
Every clinical output carries a protective header, evidence tier labels on all claims, and a mandatory Practitioner Clinical Decision section that only the treating clinician completes. Session governance records document all reasoning steps, evidence tiers applied, and practitioner decision points for regulatory compliance.
The practitioner reviews every output before it reaches the patient. VitalMatrix™ operates a practitioner review gate: the platform computes terrain intelligence, but it never acts autonomously. No output is finalised, shared, or used for clinical decisions without explicit practitioner approval. The platform amplifies clinical reasoning. It does not replace it.
