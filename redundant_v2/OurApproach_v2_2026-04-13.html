The Three Principles

Every architectural decision follows
three non-negotiable principles.

I

Terrain First

The patient's biological terrain determines the clinical picture.
Not the symptom list.

VitalMatrix™ scores seven biological nodes, aggregates them into five convergence zones, and identifies which zone is carrying the highest burden. The terrain is the starting point. Symptoms are downstream confirmation, not the primary data source.

II

Directionality Required

Knowing where the burden sits is not enough.
You need to know where it started and where it is heading.

Six cascade stacks model the directional relationships between zones. S1 traces gut dysfunction driving thyroid suppression. S2 traces metabolic energy collapse driving hormonal terrain shift. The platform computes the direction so the practitioner can intervene at the origin, not the expression.

III

Practitioner Authority

The platform computes. The practitioner decides.

VitalMatrix™ is a clinical intelligence platform. It provides terrain intelligence, cascade directionality, and Driver designation. It does not produce clinical assessments. It does not prescribe. Every output carries a mandatory Practitioner Clinical Decision section that only the treating clinician completes. The platform amplifies clinical reasoning. It never replaces it.

The Architecture

Five convergence zones. Six directional cascade stacks.

Seven biological nodes converge into five zones. Each zone carries a burden score. The zone with the highest score identifies where clinical attention is warranted first. Cascade stacks trace how burden moves between zones: direction, velocity, and evidence tier.

The Clinical Gap

Functional medicine frameworks gave practitioners the map.
It never gave them the direction of travel.

Before VitalMatrix™

The practitioner identifies which nodes are burdened. The practitioner manually estimates which zone is primary. The practitioner infers cascade direction from clinical experience and pattern recognition. The practitioner designates the Driver based on intuition refined over years of practice.

This process works. It is how functional medicine has operated since the matrix model was introduced. It is also manual, time-intensive, and impossible to scale beyond what a single practitioner can hold in working memory during a consultation.

With VitalMatrix™

Node scoring is computed from structured intake data. Zone convergence is calculated with N6 (endocrine signalling) dampened at 0.7x. Cascade directionality is modelled across six stacks with velocity and evidence tier labels. The Driver designation identifies the zone where intervention has the highest downstream cascade impact, for practitioner review.

The clinical reasoning is the same. The execution is architectural. The practitioner reviews, adjusts, and decides. The platform ensures that the depth of analysis is consistent for every patient, every time.

VitalMatrix™ generates working documents for practitioner review. All clinical decisions remain with the registered practitioner.
