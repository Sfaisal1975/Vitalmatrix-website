IMAGE STRATEGY SUMMARY

Homepage Image Approach

The homepage uses FOUR types of visual element: 1. ABSTRACT BACKGROUNDS: Radial gradients, noise textures, subtle grid patterns. No stock photography. No literal medical imagery. The dark palette IS the visual identity. 2. EMBEDDED INTERACTIVE: CascadeAtlas animated pentagon (Section 9). This is the hero visual of the entire site. Interactive on desktop, static on mobile. 3. TYPOGRAPHIC DESIGN: Gold numbers (01-06), zone colour accents, DM Mono labels. The typography system creates visual hierarchy without images. 4. SAMPLE OUTPUT MOCKUP: One redacted/blurred output document preview in Section 6 (Five Outputs). Clearly labelled as illustrative. Gives practitioners a concrete visual of what they will receive. DO NOT USE: Stock photos of doctors, stethoscopes, lab coats, DNA helixes, or any generic "medical AI" imagery. The VitalMatrix brand is defined by its dark palette, zone colours, and typographic precision. Generic imagery would undermine the premium positioning.
