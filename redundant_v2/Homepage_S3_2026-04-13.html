Commitments

Five Commitments

What we hold ourselves to. Without exception.

01

Transparent about what it is. VitalMatrix generates terrain intelligence working documents for practitioner review. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace clinical judgement.

02

Practitioner is always the authority. Every output is a draft until the practitioner reviews, edits, and approves it. The platform supports. It does not decide.

03

Evidence tier transparency. Every clinical reference includes its evidence tier. The practitioner always knows what level of evidence supports each output.

04

Protective of patient data. Registered with the ICO (ZC101813). Data governance documentation in progress. Patient data is treated as clinical infrastructure, not a product.

05

Calibrated to improve. Practitioner feedback is the primary signal for platform development. The system improves because practitioners tell it where it falls short.
