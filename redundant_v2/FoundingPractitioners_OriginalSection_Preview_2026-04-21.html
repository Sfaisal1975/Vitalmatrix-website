Cohort A and Cohort B

Cohort A

Founding Clinical Fellows

Invitation only. No charge.

Cohort A positions are offered by invitation to practitioners whose clinical profile aligns with the VitalMatrix founding architecture. There is no application process for Cohort A. If you have received an invitation, contact Dr Faisal directly.

  • No platform charge during Phase 1
  • Full co-development role
  • Direct clinical input into FLINT™ architecture

Cohort B

Founding Practitioners

Application-based. Five-year price guarantee.

Cohort B positions are open to UK functional medicine practitioners who apply for Phase 1 access. Founding Practitioners receive full platform access, a five-year price guarantee from their joining date, and a co-development role throughout Phase 1.

  • Full platform access from day one
  • Five-year price guarantee from joining date
  • Co-development role throughout Phase 1
