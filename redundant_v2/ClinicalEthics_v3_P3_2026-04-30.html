Our Five Commitments

What VitalMatrix™ Owes You

I

Transparency About What We Are

VitalMatrix™ is a clinical intelligence platform undergoing MHRA Software as a Medical Device classification assessment. We do not claim to prescribe or replace clinical judgement. We state this on every output, in every context, without exception.

01
II

The Practitioner Is Always the Authority

Every output from VitalMatrix™ carries a mandatory practitioner review requirement. The platform generates terrain intelligence: the practitioner interprets it, applies clinical judgement, and makes every clinical decision. This is not a disclaimer. It is the design.

02
III

Evidence Tier Transparency

VitalMatrix™ operates on a five-tier evidence classification system: Established, Emerging, Contested, Observed in Practice, and Theoretical. Every clinical framework is assigned a tier. We never use the phrase ‘evidence-based’ without specifying which tier applies and which source supports it.

03
IV

Data Governance

Patient data processed through VitalMatrix™ is subject to full GDPR compliance. VitalMatrix Ltd is registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO ZC101813). No patient data is used for AI training, shared with third parties, or retained beyond the purposes stated in the data processing agreement.

04
V

Commercial Transparency

The founding practitioner rate is GBP 299 per month. The 5-year price guarantee is a contractual commitment. There are no hidden fees, no automatic tier escalations, and no upsell mechanisms built into the platform.

05

Governance Questions

Questions We Want You to Ask

Practitioners applying to Phase 1 asked these. Every answer is stated policy.

VitalMatrix™ is currently undergoing MHRA Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) classification assessment. Until classification is formally confirmed, the platform operates under Phase 1 governance with mandatory protective language on all clinical outputs. We will communicate classification status transparently as it progresses.
VitalMatrix™ outputs are terrain intelligence support materials. They must be reviewed by the treating practitioner before any patient-facing use. The practitioner reviews, edits as required, and approves all output content. All outputs carry a mandatory protective header stating this requirement.
All clinical frameworks are assigned a tier from the five-tier evidence system. Contested and Theoretical tier frameworks are flagged clearly. Practitioners are never presented with contested claims without the classification explicitly stated alongside the content.
The practitioner is the final clinical authority at every stage. VitalMatrix™ is a computational support layer: the practitioner’s training, experience, and judgement govern every patient interaction. Platform outputs are advisory. All clinical decisions are made by the practitioner.
