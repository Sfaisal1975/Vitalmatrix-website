DeltaScan™ Methodology Meaningful change threshold: 8 internal points on the 0-100 internal scale. Changes below this threshold are within measurement variability and should not be reported as terrain shift. DeltaScan™ compares two complete FLINT™ pipeline runs: it is a before-and-after panel comparison, not an imaging tool. Paired sessions can be any two sessions in the patient record. Typical comparison interval: 8 to 12 weeks.

Scale note: Sliders use the internal 0-100 scale where higher values = higher burden. On the practitioner-facing display scale (0-10 inverted), a higher display score = lower burden = terrain improvement.
