Cookie Policy

VitalMatrix Ltd  |  Company No. 17046123  |  ICO Registration ZC101813  |  Last updated: April 2026

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your device and remember certain information about your visit, such as your preferences and how you interact with the site.

Similar technologies include pixel tags, web beacons, and local storage. This policy refers to all of these collectively as "cookies."

This policy explains what cookies we use on www.vitalmatrix.co.uk and within the VitalMatrix platform, why we use them, and how you can control your preferences.

We use four categories of cookies. Essential cookies are always active. All other categories require your consent.

Essential Cookies

Always active

Required for the website and platform to function correctly. They enable core features such as session management, security, and access to practitioner-only areas. The site cannot operate without these cookies. They do not collect information that identifies you for marketing purposes.

Required

Analytics Cookies

Consent required

Help us understand how visitors use our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. This enables us to measure performance and improve the site experience over time. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics cookies without your consent.

Optional

Functional Cookies

Consent required

Remember your preferences and personalise your experience, such as your preferred language, region, or display settings. These are not essential to site operation but improve usability.

Optional

Marketing Cookies

Consent required

Used to track visitors across websites to display relevant content and measure the effectiveness of campaigns. VitalMatrix does not currently operate paid advertising. This category is disclosed for transparency and completeness. No marketing cookies are set without your explicit consent.

When you first visit our website, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner. You may accept all cookies, reject optional cookies, or customise your preferences by category.

Managing Your Preferences

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of every page. Your preferences will be saved for 12 months.

Withdrawing consent for optional cookies will not affect your ability to use the core features of the VitalMatrix website. Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are required for the site to function.

You may also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, delete, and block cookies. Please note that blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of some features. Guidance for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

The VitalMatrix platform processes health and clinical data entered by registered practitioners. This data is not collected or stored via cookies. Clinical data is handled under separate data processing agreements in accordance with our Privacy Notice and applicable UK GDPR obligations for special category data.

Cookies are used only for session management, security, and analytics of general website usage. No cookies are used to store or transmit clinical or health data of any kind.
