"I fall asleep fine but wake up every night at the same time and cannot get back to sleep."

What you tell your patient "Your body does its deepest detoxification work between 1am and 3am. When that system is under strain, it wakes you up. We can map exactly where the strain is coming from and address the root cause rather than just giving you something to sleep through it."
