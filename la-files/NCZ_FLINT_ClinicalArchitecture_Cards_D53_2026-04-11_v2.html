NCZ™ CONVERGENCE ZONES

Five Zones. Five Convergence Points.

Each zone maps where biological node dysfunction converges and amplifies. The zone picture tells you where the primary burden is sitting.

Z1
Zone 1

Metabolic Energy Axis

Adrenal · Thyroid · Pancreatic

"Where is the metabolic burden concentrated?"

N3 Energy N6 (endocrine signalling) Threshold: 40 TerrainLock™

The metabolic core. Energy production and endocrine communication converge across adrenal, thyroid, and pancreatic subsystems. Two nodes. Often the first zone to show burden in complex chronic presentations. Participates in the TerrainLock™ loop: Z2 to Z1 to Z5 to Z2.

Zone 2

Resilience Network

Gut · Immune · Brain

"Where is the immune-gut-neural convergence?"

N1 Assimilation N2 Defence and Repair N6 (endocrine signalling) Threshold: 40 TerrainLock™

The gut-immune-defence layer. Three nodes. Default cascade entry point and the most common tiebreaker zone in complex presentations. TerrainLock™ self-perpetuating loop originates here: Z2 to Z1 to Z5 to Z2. Z2 gut restoration is the default tiebreaker.

Zone 3

Cardiovascular-Neural Axis

Cardiac · Pulmonary · Neurovascular

"Where is transport and neural flow compromised?"

N5 Transport N6 (endocrine signalling) Threshold: 40

The vascular-neural shift. Transport replaces Energy: N3 exits, N5 enters. Z3 marks the shift from metabolic terrain to cardiovascular and neural flow. Receives cascade from Z1 (S6, unidirectional: Z1 to Z3 only) and from Z2 (S5). Z3 is not ready for composite scoring in Phase 1.

Zone 4

Detoxification Trident

Hepatic · Lymphatic · Renal

"Where is biotransformation capacity under strain?"

N2 Defence and Repair N4 Biotransformation and Elimination Threshold: 40

The only zone with no N6 (endocrine signalling). Z4 breaks the pattern deliberately: pure detox and defence pair. Two nodes. Upstream activity at Z4 precipitates downstream cascade into Z1 via S3 (Slow). Z4 is not ready for composite scoring in Phase 1.

Zone 5

Hormonal Terrain Axis

Androgenic · Estrogenic · Progestogenic

"Where is the hormonal terrain under pressure?"

N3 Energy N4 Biotransformation and Elimination N6 (endocrine signalling) Threshold: 32 (lower) TerrainLock™

The most complex zone. Three nodes. Energy returns, Biotransformation joins, endocrine signalling completes. Activates at a lower threshold (32 internal) than Z1 to Z4 (40 internal): hormonal terrain is sensitive. Near-threshold window: 5 internal points below threshold. Almost always a secondary burden zone, rarely the highest burden zone. Participates in TerrainLock™: Z2 to Z1 to Z5 to Z2.

FLINT™ Terrain Intelligence Layers

FLINT™ FIVE LAYERS

Five Layers. Five Clinical Questions.

Each FLINT™ layer answers a specific clinical question. The layers execute in sequence from L1 through L5.

L1
Layer 1: Node Intelligence

WHERE?

"Where is dysfunction concentrated across the seven biological nodes?"

MSQ-71 ATM MedTerrain™ 7 Nodes N6 dampened

Maps dysfunction across seven biological nodes: N1 Assimilation, N2 Defence and Repair, N3 Energy, N4 Biotransformation and Elimination, N5 Transport, N6 (endocrine signalling) dampened, and N7 Structural Integrity (Phase 2). Internal scale 0 to 100. Display scale 0 to 10 inverted.

MES: Metabolic Environment and Substrate: foundational context. Not scored. Not a node.
L2
Layer 2: Zone Intelligence

WHICH?

"Which convergence zones are active?"

NCZ™ Composite N6 dampened Z1-Z4: threshold 40 Z5: threshold 32

Identifies which of the five NCZ™ convergence zones are active. Zone scores aggregate from constituent node scores with N6 (endocrine signalling) dampened. Z5 activates at 32 (lower threshold reflecting hormonal sensitivity). Near-threshold window: 5 internal points below each zone threshold.

L3
Layer 3: Cascade Intelligence

HOW?

"How are the active zones connected, and in which direction is dysfunction moving?"

CascadeIQ™ 6 Stacks KINETICS™ TerrainLock™ Detection

Six directional cascade stacks map zone-to-zone dysfunction flow. S1: Z2 to Z1, Medium, Mechanism Established / Cascade Emerging. S2: Z1 to Z5, Fast, Established. S3: Z4 to Z1, Slow, Emerging. S5: Z2 to Z3, Fast, Emerging. S6: Z1 to Z3, Medium, Established, unidirectional: Z1 to Z3 only. TerrainLock™ detection: Z2 to Z1 to Z5 to Z2 self-perpetuating loop.

S4: Z5 to Z2 · Theoretical · Medium S6: Unidirectional Z1→Z3

S4 Theoretical: displays but is excluded from DRD™ cascade tracing (D-12). Clinical decisions based on S4 alone require practitioner confirmation.

L4
Layer 4: Causality Intelligence

WHO?

"Which zone is carrying the primary burden?"

DRD™ Highest Burden Zone Z2 Tiebreaker

DRD™ formally designates the highest burden zone: the zone originating the terrain cascade. Designation follows Established and Emerging cascade stacks only. Theoretical pathways (S4) display but are excluded from the cascade trace (D-12). The most symptomatic zone is often not the originating zone. Z2 is the default tiebreaker.

L5
Layer 5: Examination Intelligence

WHAT?

"What should be investigated first, and in what order?"

APEX™ PRISM™ 3 Budget Tiers

APEX™ (Assessment Priority and Evidence Xray) sequences examination priorities by causality, not severity. Investigation order follows the highest burden zone designation from L4. Three budget tiers: GBP 200 to 400 (Tier 1), GBP 400 to 800 (Tier 2), GBP 800+ (Tier 3). DeltaScan™ is the sixth clinical stage (follow-up layer): not a FLINT™ layer.
