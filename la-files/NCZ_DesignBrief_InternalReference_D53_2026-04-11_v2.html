The Fundamental Difference
Not a static shape with a number.
A living convergence diagram.

Every competitor zone system shows a coloured shape (triangle, circle, hexagon) with a number inside and a name below. Static. Illustrative. Decorative.


VitalMatrix™ shows something clinically accurate and visually unprecedented: an actual node convergence diagram. This is not a metaphor. This is what the NCZ™ model actually describes: three physiological systems converging at a clinical intersection point. The visual matches the science.
