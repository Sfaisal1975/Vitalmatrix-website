Androgenic · Estrogenic · Progestogenic

ES (N6) BE (N4) EN (N3) Endocrine Signalling Biotransformation TerrainLock™

The hormonal terrain axis integrates endocrine signalling, biotransformation, and energy regulation across three hormonal subsystems.
