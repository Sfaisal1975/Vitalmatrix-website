TerrainLock™: Self-Perpetuating Cascade Loop

Z2 → Z1 → Z5 → Z2

TerrainLock™ describes a self-perpetuating cascade loop within the NCZ™ (Node-Cascade Zone™) architecture. When cascade stacks S1 (Z2→Z1), S2 (Z1→Z5), and S4 (Z5→Z2) are simultaneously active, a closed directional circuit forms: Z2 drives Z1, Z1 drives Z5, and Z5 feeds back to Z2. This loop creates a reinforcing cycle where dysfunction in any participating zone sustains dysfunction in the others, making clinical resolution more difficult without addressing the loop as a whole. S4 is classified as Theoretical (D-12), meaning TerrainLock™ activation is architecturally modelled but clinically uncommon.
