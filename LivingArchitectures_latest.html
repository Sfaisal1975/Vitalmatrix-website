TerrainLock Detection

The Self-Perpetuating Loop

Z2 → Z1 → Z5 → Z2. When all three cascade stacks activate simultaneously, the terrain enters a self-reinforcing cycle. Z2 is the default tiebreaker entry point.

TerrainLock Inactive
S4 is always labelled THEORETICAL. Activation is architecturally possible but clinically uncommon in Phase 1. Do not present as routinely firing. S4 (Z5 to Z2) is excluded from DRD cascade trace (D-12). Z2 is the default tiebreaker entry point.
