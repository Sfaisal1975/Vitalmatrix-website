2 What Data This Website Collects

Contact form submissions: your name, email address, and the message you submit when you use our contact form.

Newsletter sign-up: your email address and, if provided, your name and professional role.

Analytics data: technical data about how visitors use this website, collected in aggregated or pseudonymised form using cookieless tracking. It does not identify you as an individual.

We do not collect patient health data on this website. Patient data is processed only within the VitalMatrix platform under a separate legal framework.

3 Legal Basis for Processing

We only process your personal data where we have a lawful basis under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Contact form submissions: Article 6(1)(a), your consent.

Newsletter sign-ups: Article 6(1)(a), your consent. You may unsubscribe at any time.

Analytics data: Article 6(1)(f), our legitimate interests. Analytics is aggregated and pseudonymised.

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making as defined under Articles 22A to 22D of the UK GDPR (as substituted by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, s.80).

4 How Long We Keep Your Data

Cookie Policy

1 What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. Non-essential cookies require your consent under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR).

2 Cookies on This Website

Strictly necessary cookies: essential for the website to function (session management, security, hosting). No consent required.

Analytics: VitalMatrix uses Matomo, self-hosted within the EU (Hetzner Cloud, Germany), configured for cookieless tracking. No analytics cookies are placed on your device.

Marketing cookies: this website does not use marketing or advertising cookies.

3 Managing Cookies

Because our analytics uses cookieless tracking, the only cookies are strictly necessary ones from the hosting provider. All major browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings.
