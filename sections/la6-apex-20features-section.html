Prioritised Investigation. Three Budget Tiers.

APEX™: Assessment Priority and Evidence Xray. Twenty interactive components demonstrating budget-tiered investigation planning with evidence tiers, cascade integration, and practitioner workflow tools. T-01 compliant.

Feature 1

Cascade-Linked Investigation Sequencing

Select the Driver. APEX shows cascade-connected zones and recommends investigation order: source zone first, then downstream reactors via active stacks.

Feature 2

Live Budget Accumulator with Shortlist

Tick investigations to build a shortlist. Running total updates in real time. Budget ceiling lines appear at tier boundaries.

Selected: GBP 0
SELECTED INVESTIGATIONS

Feature 3

Evidence Confidence Heatmap

Grid view showing evidence density across all investigations for the selected zone. Brighter cells indicate stronger evidence clustering.

Feature 4

Cross-Zone Investigation Overlap Detector

Investigations that appear in multiple zones are flagged as dual-yield. One test, multiple zones covered. Potential cost savings highlighted.

Feature 5

Tier Progression Waterfall

Click Tier 1 results to mark as flagged or within range. Tier 2 and Tier 3 investigations unlock conditionally based on Tier 1 findings.

Feature 6

Functional Optimal Range Lens

Click any investigation to reveal conventional vs functional optimal ranges. The narrower functional window is where terrain intelligence focuses.

Feature 7

Investigation Dependency Chain

Some tests are redundant when a comprehensive panel is ordered. Selecting the full panel auto-dims covered individual tests.

Feature 8

Practitioner Annotation Sticky Notes

Click the pin icon to attach a free-text note to any investigation. Notes persist within the session. Documentation layer only.

Feature 9

Seasonal and Circadian Specimen Timing Advisor

Optimal collection windows, fasting requirements, and seasonal considerations for each investigation type. Logistical intelligence, not clinical.

Feature 10

Budget Scenario Comparator

Side-by-side comparison of two budget scenarios. See what additional spend buys in terms of zone coverage and evidence strength.

Scenario A: Tier 1 Only

Scenario B: Tier 1 + Tier 2

Feature 11

Cascade Source Priority Marker

DRD designates the Driver. APEX reorders zone tabs: source zone first with a pulsing gold ring. Reactor zones show their stack connection.

Feature 12

Evidence Tier Confidence Trajectory

Micro-sparklines show how evidence strength for each test has shifted over recent years. Rising trajectories indicate growing confidence.

Feature 13

Patient Cost Transparency Card

Toggle to patient-friendly view: plain English names, single midpoint costs, one-sentence explanations. Suitable for screen-sharing during consultations.

Feature 14

Multi-Zone Basket Consolidation

Investigations from all active zones merged into a single list, grouped by specimen type. Duplicates collapsed with zone badges.

Feature 15

Lab Provider Cost Comparison Grid

Three price tiers per investigation: NHS pathway, private lab standard, specialist functional lab. Horizontal bars show cost spectrum. No lab named.

Indicative pricing only. Verify with your preferred laboratory.

Feature 16

Investigation Impact Radar

Hover over any investigation to see a mini pentagon radar showing which zones that single test provides data for. High-value tests light multiple axes.

Feature 17

Borderline Zone Investigation Escalation

Zones within the D-38 near-threshold window (5 internal points below activation) show confirmation-focused framing. Tier 3 dimmed unless Tier 1 supports escalation.

Feature 18

Historical Investigation Tracker

Timeline showing investigation shortlists from previous DeltaScan timepoints. REPEAT, NEW, and DROPPED badges reveal how the diagnostic strategy evolved.

Feature 19

Cascade-Weighted Cost Efficiency Score

Each investigation scored by cost divided by number of zones it informs. Sort by efficiency to find the best diagnostic value per pound across the terrain.

Feature 20

Regulatory Provenance Watermark

Hover any investigation to reveal its evidence source. Filter by evidence tier. Transparent provenance accountability built into the interaction surface.

All evidence tiers assigned per D-05 five-tier classification. No Contested-tier investigations included in Phase 1 demonstration.
