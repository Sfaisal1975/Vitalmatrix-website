This Is Not a Beta Test

We are not asking you to subscribe to a finished product.

We are asking you to sit at the table while the most important decisions about how this platform works are still being made. Founding practitioners shape the clinical logic, the interface, and the vocabulary of how VitalMatrix™ reaches the market.

Five practitioners will hold Cohort A status as Founding Clinical Fellows. Ten total founding positions are available across both cohorts. We would like you to be among the first.

The platform is functional. The architecture is locked. What is still being refined is the clinical workflow: how practitioners move through it, what they see first, and how the output lands in a real consultation. That refinement requires practitioners who will engage with it critically.

The Gap No One Has Filled

Without terrain intelligence, the gap has real consequences.

01

It Costs You Time

Constructing a directional clinical hypothesis manually from complex multi-system data is one of the most time-consuming elements of functional medicine practice. VitalMatrix™ structures this process systematically, so you spend consultation time on clinical reasoning and patient relationships, not data assembly.

02

It Costs You Confidence

In complex cases with multiple active systems, even experienced practitioners encounter clinical uncertainty about where to start. VitalMatrix™ provides a structured, reproducible analytical output that supports clinical confidence with documented, auditable reasoning.

03

It Costs Your Patients

A practitioner who begins intervention in a secondary system rather than the primary burden-driving system may see slow progress, patient frustration, and eventual attrition. The patient is not failing to respond. The clinical sequence was simply not optimised.

04

It Costs the Profession

Functional medicine's case for clinical rigour depends on its ability to document, measure, and reproduce clinical outcomes. Without structured terrain intelligence tools, practitioners cannot easily demonstrate the systematic clinical reasoning that underpins their methodology.

Selection Criteria

Who We Are Looking For

Founding practitioners are selected personally by Dr Faisal. This is not a first-come subscription. Applications are reviewed against the following criteria:

  • UK-based private practice: Phase 1 is UK only.
  • Functional medicine training: IFM Matrix or equivalent terrain-based methodology.
  • Complex chronic patient caseload: five to twenty complex cases per week minimum.
  • Genuine interest in shaping the platform, not just using it.

If you are unsure whether you meet the criteria, apply anyway. The criteria describe the ideal founding practitioner. The final decision is a conversation, not a filter.
