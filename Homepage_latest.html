Platform Positioning

The gap VitalMatrix™ was purpose-built to close.

Three angles on the same verified truth. Each card states what no competitive landscape audit contradicts.

The question

Purpose-built for the question functional medicine has never had infrastructure to answer.

Other platforms in the functional medicine market have focused on documentation, EHR management, evidence retrieval, and node organisation. None of them are built to answer the sequencing question: which physiological system is driving the cascade, and in what order clinical attention should move. VitalMatrix™ is purpose-built for that question. Not as a feature. As the architecture.

The distinction

Measuring biological burden is a solved problem. Knowing which burden is driving the others is not.

The functional medicine market has produced excellent tools for measuring terrain burden. Biomarker panels, node scoring, MSQ severity maps. What those tools produce is a picture of what is burdened. What they cannot produce is the sequencing answer: which of the burdened systems is upstream, which are reacting to it, and in what order they should be addressed. VitalMatrix™ produces that answer via CascadeIQ™ and DRD™.

The infrastructure

Functional medicine has the tools to measure everything. VitalMatrix™ is the infrastructure to sequence it.

DUTCH, organic acids, micronutrient panels, gastrointestinal mapping: the measurement infrastructure available to UK functional medicine practitioners is extraordinary. What has been absent is the analytical layer that turns all of it into terrain intelligence. VitalMatrix™ is that layer. Not a replacement for the measurement tools. The computational infrastructure that turns measurement into prioritised terrain insight.

Clinical Philosophy

The principles behind the platform.

Four commitments that define how VitalMatrix™ approaches terrain intelligence. Each one is a choice about what the platform is not.

4 Architectural Principles

01 / PRINCIPLE

Terrain, not targets

The platform does not identify pathogens, deficiencies, or clinical conditions. It maps biological terrain: the functional state of seven interconnected systems and the Node-Cascade Zones where burden accumulates. Terrain intelligence identifies where to look. Clinical judgement determines what to do.

02 / PRINCIPLE

Direction, not description

Other platforms describe what is present. VitalMatrix™ designates direction: which zone is driving the others, which systems are reacting downstream, and which cascade pathways connect them. Directional intelligence is the difference between treating the loudest signal and treating its source.

03 / PRINCIPLE

Sequence, not simultaneity

The platform does not produce a list of everything to investigate. It sequences investigation by cascade position: confirm the highest burden zone first, then trace downstream impact. Sequenced investigation is more efficient, more cost-effective, and more clinically coherent than parallel testing.

04 / PRINCIPLE

Governance, not autonomy

Every output includes an evidence tier label, a practitioner override option, and a blank Practitioner Clinical Decision section. VitalMatrix™ generates working documents for practitioner review. The clinical decision, always, belongs to the registered practitioner.

Is VitalMatrix MHRA approved?
MHRA approval awaited. VitalMatrix is currently classified as a documentation and terrain intelligence tool, not a diagnostic device. All outputs are working documents for practitioner review. The regulatory pathway is being managed with specialist legal counsel (Browne Jacobson).
What happens to patient data?
VitalMatrix is registered with the ICO (ZC101813). Patient data is never used to train external models. Data processing is governed by UK GDPR Article 28. The platform does not share, sell, or expose patient data to third parties.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes. There is no lock-in period. Founding practitioners receive a 14-day free trial and a 28-day money-back guarantee. You can cancel at any point after that with no penalty. The five-year rate guarantee applies for as long as you remain subscribed.
Does it work with my existing clinical system?
VitalMatrix operates as a standalone terrain intelligence platform. Outputs can be exported and placed into your existing patient record system. Direct EMR integration is on the Phase 2 roadmap. The platform does not require you to change your existing clinical workflow.
What training is required?
The platform is designed for practitioners already trained in functional medicine frameworks. If you understand node-based clinical reasoning, the platform will be intuitive. Every founding practitioner receives personal onboarding with Dr Faisal.
How is this different from general-purpose AI tools?
General-purpose AI applies conventional reference ranges and requires manual correction. VitalMatrix uses a purpose-built terrain architecture (seven nodes, five zones, six cascade stacks) designed specifically for functional medicine. It maps burden direction, not just symptom presence.
