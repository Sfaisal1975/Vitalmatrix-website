## 1. Overview

The FLINT overlay architecture is a cross-cutting meta-intelligence layer that sits above the five-layer FLINT engine (D-78 Option B). It is **not a sixth FLINT layer** and is never described as one. It comprises four outputs and supporting components, all governed by W26 Clinical Architecture under SA authority.

**What the overlay does:** The FLINT engine produces zone scores, cascade detection, DRD designation, and APEX priorities. The overlay synthesises these outputs into clinical intelligence the practitioner can act on.

**Governing authority:** ALB v1.6 (D-62) plus D-85, D-86, D-87, D-130, D-134, D-149. VRB v1.1 Active Mode. W26 Clinical Architecture.

**Operative rulings as at 13 May 2026:** D-121 to D-129 (overlay mnemonic set), D-131 (GRADE), D-132 (NEXUS), D-133 (TerrainSpiral), D-135 (MODES expansion), D-154 (VOLTERRAIN Tier 3), D-164 (D5 SPRA), D-165 (HPA-vagus + NEXUS + MOD-030/WS2.4), D-170 (collision remediation), D-178 (OQ-SL qualified syntax), D-179 (TerrainSpiral Phase 1), D-180 (IC39 Phase 1), D-181 (Synthesis Layer v1.0 conditional sign-off).

**INTERNAL WORKING LABELS ONLY** per D-179. These names do not appear in any practitioner-facing, investor-facing, or external-boundary communication.

| Trajectory State | Clinical Meaning |
|-----------------|------------------|
| **Stabilising** | Terrain showing significant burden reduction or stable hold across reviewed zones. Primary burden zone improving. Terrain is responding. Continue and monitor. |

### 5c. Distinction from TerrainLock

| | TerrainLock | TerrainSpiral |
|--|------------|---------------|
| **What it is** | A STATE: terrain locked in a self-perpetuating loop. | A TRAJECTORY: terrain moving in a direction over time. |

### 11b. The Four-Cause Differential

Triggered after two sessions without improvement. Practitioner reviews against four causes:

| # | Cause | Review |
|---|-------|--------|
| 1 | **Substrate adequacy reviewed** | Are the basic terrain support inputs sufficient? Magnesium status, B vitamins, omega-3 ratio, hydration, sleep architecture. |
| 2 | **Hidden driver screening completed** | Is a VOLTERRAIN driver active that was not in the initial driver inventory? Mycotoxin exposure, EMF burden, gut pathogen, oral cavity infection, parasympathetic burden. |
| 3 | **Compliance verified** | Is the patient executing the terrain support direction as agreed? Compliance gap is itself a clinical signal worth treating, not a moral failure to flag. |
| 4 | **Archetype re-check** | Was the original terrain configuration pattern accurate? Patients often present mixed-pattern signals. Re-assessment after two sessions of non-response can reveal a different configuration pattern was the operative one. |

**Architectural distinction from CADENCE:** IC39 and CADENCE are architecturally distinct mechanisms (D-180 FM2 preventive strategy). IC39 is practitioner-mediated adaptation. CADENCE is algorithmic self-modification. They are not on a continuous scale and must not be presented as a continuous development pathway in any investor or practitioner communication.
